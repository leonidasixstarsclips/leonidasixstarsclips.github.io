What is SixStarsClips

SixStarsClips is a fan-run YouTube channel. Every week it publishes a six-minute compilation of the funniest and most spectacular GTA 6 moments, taken from public Twitch clips. Every clip is numbered, narrated and credited to its creator on screen for its whole duration and in the description, with a link to their channel. Any streamer can ask to have their clips removed or excluded, and we act promptly.

Viewers comment their favorite clip, and the week's top 3 becomes a YouTube Short.

How the application works

The SixStarsClips application is an internal tool used only by the channel owner. It:

  • collects the week's most viewed public GTA 6 clips (Twitch API), excluding streamers who asked not to be featured;
  • edits the weekly video after the owner manually reviews and approves every clip;
  • uploads the video and the Short to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services;
  • posts a comment on the channel's own videos and reads their public comments to see which clips viewers liked most.

The application does not access anyone else's Google account and does not ask any visitor to sign in. Details about the data it processes: Privacy Policy.
